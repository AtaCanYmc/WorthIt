# 📱 Smart Shopping Assistant - App Store Specification

## 🚀 App Title
**Smart Deal: Pizza, Coffee & Market Compare**

## 📝 Short Description
Stop overpaying! Instantly find the best value for Pizzas, Coffee sizes, and Supermarket products with one tap. Calculate, compare, and save money in any currency or unit.

---

## ⭐ Key Features

*   **🍕 Pizza Saver**: Don't be fooled by sizes! Compare 2 smaller pizzas vs. 1 large one instantly based on diameter and price. Supports both **cm** and **Inches**.
*   **☕ Coffee Value**: Tall, Grande, or Venti? Find out which size gives you the most caffeine for your cash. Auto-adjusts to **ml** or **fl oz**.
*   **🛒 Market Genius**: Compare any two products (e.g., 500g pack vs. 1kg pack) to see the true unit price and catch hidden price hikes.
*   **🌍 Truly Global**:
    *   **Multi-Language**: Fully localized in English, Turkish, German, and French.
    *   **Multi-Currency**: Works with USD ($), EUR (€), TRY (₺), and GBP (£).
    *   **Unit Systems**: Seamlessly switch between Metric (European standards) and Imperial (US standards) at any time.
*   **🌙 Eye-Friendly**: Sleek, modern Dark Mode implementation for night-time shopping.

---

## 🌍 Why This App?

**"Europe, USA, or Anywhere!"**
Whether you are shopping for groceries in Berlin using Euros and Grams, or ordering a pizza in New York using Dollars and Inches, **Smart Deal** adapts to you. It's the only shopping calculator that travels with you, offering instant conversion and comparison logic natively.

## 💡 The Problem & Solution

**The Trap**: Marketing tricks often make it impossible to know if the "Family Size" or "Economy Pack" is actually a deal. Is a 12-inch pizza really twice as big as an 8-inch? (Spoiler: It's more than double!).

**The Solution**: **Smart Deal** cuts through the marketing noise. It calculates the *real* math behind the price tags in seconds. We help you shop with confidence, ensuring you never pay more for less again. Your pocket—and your brain—will thank you!
